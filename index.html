What people come for

Most visitors split their time between the beaches, the rainforest, and the volcano.

Yellow Leaf Bay

White sand beaches encircle the bay, a short walk from central Taniti City.

The volcano

Taniti's small active volcano sits in the mountainous interior. Guided hikes daily.

The rainforest

Hiking trails, river tours, and zip-lines through the canopy.

Merriton Landing

The fast-growing north side of the bay: pubs, a microbrewery, bowling, and a dance club.

Good to know before you come

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar, and many businesses also take euros and yen. Power outlets are 120 volts, the same as the United States. Taniti City is flat and very walkable, and public buses run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day.

The island keeps a large number of national holidays, and many attractions and restaurants close for them, so it's worth checking dates before you book. More about the island
