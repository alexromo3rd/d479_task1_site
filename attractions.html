Attractions

Every licensed guide and operator on the island, in one place.

Volcano hike

$65 per person

4 hours · 7:00 am and 1:00 pm daily · 12 people per group

A guided climb into the mountainous interior to Taniti's small active volcano. Moderate difficulty — expect loose rock near the top. Water and a walking stick are included; bring closed shoes.

Check dates

Rainforest river tour

$40 per person

2 hours · Four departures daily · 8 people per boat

Upriver into the rainforest, with stops at two waterfalls. Good for families — the water is flat the whole route and life vests are provided in all sizes.

Check dates

Yellow Leaf Bay snorkeling

$55 per person

Half day · Mornings only · Gear included

A boat trip to the reef inside the bay. Masks, fins, and a guide are included, and the boat carries a spotter for first-timers. Mornings are when the water is clearest.

Check dates

Chartered fishing tour

$120 per person

6 hours · Departs from the harbor at 6:00 am · 6 people per boat

Out past the reef with a local crew. Rods, bait, and lunch are included, and the crew will clean and bag your catch at the dock. Restaurants in Taniti City will cook it for you the same evening.

Check dates

Island bus tour

$35 per person

3 hours · Tuesdays, Thursdays, and Saturdays · 9:00 am

A loop of the island on the coast road, with stops at the rocky north beaches, a lookout over the interior, and the harbor. A good first day if you want to get your bearings before booking anything else.

Check dates

Merriton Landing evening walk

$30 per person

2 hours · Nightly at 6:00 pm · 15 people per group

A guided walk through the new development on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay, ending at the microbrewery. Tastings are included for guests 18 and over. The whole area is easy to explore on foot afterwards.
